## Product Truth Reviewed

The connected WCL sources show two complementary implementations:

- The Base44 app exposes authenticated routes for campus, courses, course learning, tutor, performing arts, residence, coding lab, exam center, adaptive learning, dashboard, profile, glossary, instructors, community, and admin.
- The GitHub cinematic vertical slice documents a photographic campus entrance, enterable lecture hall/library/music/ballet/jazz spaces, an AI Foundations course player, notes, bookmarks, progress, knowledge checks, read-aloud controls, a local Windows speech fallback, an animated professor whiteboard, and explicit boundaries around what still requires configured live providers or later phases.

The product is explicitly a **private, non-accredited educational and study system** and should not be marketed as awarding accredited degrees, licenses, or professional certification.

## Customer Truth

Most AI education products look like another content library. Wilkerson AI University has a more differentiated experiential story: learning is organized around a place — campus, lecture, library, lab, course progress, and guided study — rather than a pile of disconnected videos and bookmarks.

## Creative

### Hook

> Stop opening another AI course tab. Open a campus.

### Social / Landing Copy

Wilkerson AI University is being built as a private AI learning environment you **enter**, not just a playlist you consume.

Walk into the campus experience. Open a course. Take notes. Bookmark what matters. Track progress. Use read-aloud. Move between the lecture hall, library, coding lab, performing arts, and study tools.

The goal is not to pretend a personal learning system is an accredited university. The goal is to make self-directed AI education feel organized, immersive, and worth returning to.

**Knowledge. Wisdom. Innovation. Legacy.**
