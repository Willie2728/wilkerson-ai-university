# RUN121 — Wilkerson AI University

## Customer Truth
A learning receipt is stronger when it says how much AI assistance was allowed.

A learner can complete an AI-assisted task without knowing which parts they can explain, modify, or reproduce independently. RUN121 makes the assistance boundary explicit before the proof step.

## Creative Strategy
**Hook:** If the AI did the hard part, say so—then reduce the help on the next attempt.

**CTA:** Set an AI-assistance boundary, choose a proof step, and copy a Learning Receipt.
